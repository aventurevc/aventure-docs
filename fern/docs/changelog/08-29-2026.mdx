## Search From a Shared Article or Page URL

`GET /v1/search/link` takes a URL that someone shared (an article, a company
page) and resolves it against aVenture's entity, person, and news records.

## Update a Person's Address in Place

`PUT` and `PATCH` on `/v1/people/{personId}/addresses/{addressJoinId}` replace or
amend an existing person-address record. Previously the join had to be deleted
and recreated.

## Internal Endpoints Removed From the Public Surface

Health, readiness, liveness, service info, and the CLI authorization and
personal-API-key endpoints are no longer part of the published specification.
They were operational plumbing rather than integration surface. If you were
calling them, move to the documented `/v1` endpoints.
