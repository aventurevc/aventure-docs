## What Counts

- **Company and person profile views** count one view per record, per calendar
  month (UTC). Reading the same record again that month is free.
- **Company brand lookups** count one brand view per company, per calendar
  month (UTC), on a separate allowance from profile views; the
  [company brand lookup](/company-brand-lookup) lists each plan's amount.
- **Web searches** count one each.
- **Plain-English searches** have no monthly allowance. The free plan includes
  a daily allowance of them; past it, they answer `402` with `code`
  `subscription_required`. Every plan is also rate-limited per account: a `429`
  with `limitType` `NATURAL_SEARCH` means wait the `Retry-After` seconds, not
  upgrade.
- **Research requests** count against the allowance for adding or updating a
  company or person. Research requires a paid plan that includes research requests.
  Prepaid credit only extends that plan's allowance when additional usage is on.
- **Features outside your plan**, such as link search or page reads on the
  free plan, answer `402` with `code` `subscription_required`. Buying credit
  does not unlock paid features or remove the free plan's daily caps on
  plain-English searches and name lookups.

Allowances reset at the start of each calendar month (UTC).

## When You Reach a Limit

When an allowance runs out, the API answers `429` with `code`
`billing_allowance_exhausted`. The message names the limit you reached and how
to upgrade. Upgrade in either of two places:

- **Website**: [subscription settings](https://aventure.vc/settings/subscription)
  shows your plan, your usage, and every plan you can move to.
- **CLI**: `aventure billing plans list` lists plans with monthly and annual
  prices. `aventure billing plan-changes create --plan <plan>` upgrades a paid
  plan using the card already on file. From the free plan,
  `aventure billing checkout-sessions create --plan <plan>` returns a checkout
  link where you enter a card.

Over MCP, your assistant can list aVenture plans, but it cannot change your
plan; upgrade on the website or with the CLI.

If an upgrade needs a payment you have not made yet, the response includes a
payment link to finish it. You can also turn on additional usage in
[subscription settings](https://aventure.vc/settings/subscription) to continue
eligible requests past their allowance, subject to your monthly usage cap if set.
`PREPAID_CREDIT` uses purchased credit without a subscription; `USAGE_INVOICE`
requires an eligible paid Stripe plan. Credit does not change plan entitlements.

Buy prepaid credit on the website or through `POST /v1/billing/credit-purchases`.
Choose a pack or an amount from 500 to 100000 USD cents. `HOSTED_CHECKOUT` opens
a payment page; `ATTACHED_CARD` charges the existing default saved card. Send a
required `Idempotency-Key` header; retrying with the same key and identical
terms resumes the same payment.
`GET /v1/billing/credit-purchases/{purchaseId}` returns its status.

For a delegated purchase, such as an OAuth or API-key call by an assistant,
the user must first enable assistant purchasing in web settings. The request
must preserve the user's exact `authorizationQuote` verbatim and include an
explicit `capDecision` to keep, set (`SET_CAP` with `spendCapCents`), or clear
the monthly usage cap. The cap
limits usage, not the credit purchase. Delegated changes to additional usage
settings also require permission, and the user's exact authorization quote
when they turn usage on or change funding or limits.
