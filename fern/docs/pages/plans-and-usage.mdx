API, CLI, and MCP calls all draw on the same account. Upgrade on the
[pricing page](https://aventure.vc/pricing).

## Plans

| Tier | Price | Company research requests / mo | Person research requests / mo | Company profile views / mo | Person profile views / mo | Web searches / mo | Brand lookups / mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Essential (free) | $0 | 0 | 0 | 25 | 25 | 25 | 250 |
| Plus | $49/mo or $490/yr | 30 | 30 | 500 | 500 | 200 | 5,000 |
| Pro | $199/mo or $1,999/yr | 270 | 270 | 5,000 | 5,000 | 1,000 | 50,000 |
| Expert | contact sales | custom reports, CRM integration, dedicated analyst | | | | | |

- The free plan includes 20 name identifications (lookups) per day; more
  need Plus or Pro.
- Plus adds plain-English questions and name lookup in the API, CLI, and MCP
  server, competitor, provider, and investor-activity questions, the Analysis
  tab (SWOT, Five Forces, Customer Acquisition, research memo), saved lists,
  unlimited search results, and industry briefs.
- Pay-as-you-go top-ups: credit packs of $25, $50, $100, and $250. Overage
  unit prices: company research $2.50, person research $0.65, profile view
  $0.025, web search $0.025.
- The same sign-in works on the website, the iPhone, iPad, and Mac apps, the
  CLI (`aventure`, npm `@aventurevc/aventure-cli`), and the MCP server
  (`@aventurevc/mcp-server`, hosted at `https://mcp.aventure.vc/mcp`).

## What Counts

- **Company and person profile views** count one view per record, per calendar
  month (UTC). Reading the same record again that month is free.
- **Company brand lookups** count one brand view per company, per calendar
  month (UTC), on a separate allowance from profile views; the
  [company brand lookup](/company-brand-lookup) lists each plan's amount.
- **Web searches** count one each.
- **Plain-English searches** have no monthly allowance. They are rate-limited
  per account instead: a `429` with `limitType` `NATURAL_SEARCH` means wait the
  `Retry-After` seconds, not upgrade.
- **Research requests** count against the allowance for adding or updating a
  company or person.
- **Features outside your plan**, such as plain-English search on a plan that
  does not include it, answer `402` with `code` `subscription_required`.

Allowances reset at the start of each calendar month (UTC).

## When You Reach a Limit

When an allowance runs out, the API answers `429` with `code`
`billing_allowance_exhausted`. The message names the limit you reached and how
to upgrade. Upgrade in either of two places:

- **Website**: [subscription settings](https://aventure.vc/settings/subscription)
  shows your plan, your usage, and every plan you can move to.
- **CLI**: `aventure billing plans list` lists plans with monthly and annual
  prices. `aventure billing plan-changes create --plan <plan>` upgrades a paid
  plan using the card already on file. From the free plan,
  `aventure billing checkout-sessions create --plan <plan>` returns a checkout
  link where you enter a card.

Over MCP, your assistant can list aVenture plans, but it cannot change your
plan; upgrade on the website or with the CLI.

If an upgrade needs a payment you have not made yet, the response includes a
payment link to finish it. On plans that offer additional usage, you can turn it
on so requests continue past the allowance up to a spending cap you set.
