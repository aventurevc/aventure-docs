## Add a Custom Connector in Grok

On Grok Business or Enterprise, ask a team admin to [add aVenture to your team's connectors](https://docs.x.ai/grok/connector-management). Once they add it, open [Grok Connectors](https://grok.com/connectors), choose **aVenture**, and skip to step 3.

1. Open [Grok Connectors](https://grok.com/connectors). Choose **New Connector → Custom**.
2. Enter `https://mcp.aventure.vc/mcp` and name the connector **aVenture** if asked.
3. Sign in to aVenture and approve the connection. You can use a [free aVenture account](https://aventure.vc/sign-up) without an API key. Return to Grok and open a chat.

[Grok's connector guide](https://docs.x.ai/grok/connectors) covers personal and team setup.
