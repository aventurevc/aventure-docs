plan works.

## What the Data Covers

| Records | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Companies](/api-reference/entities/list-entities) | Companies, investors, and accelerators, filterable by industry, stage, location, funding, and cohort |
| [People](/api-reference/people/search-people) | Founders and investors, their roles, and their investments |
| [Funding rounds](/api-reference/entity-fundraise/list-entity-fundraise-rounds) | Round, amount raised, valuation, and investors |
| [News](/api-reference/news/list-news) | Articles linked to the companies and people they mention |

[Search](/api-reference/search/federated) queries all four record types in one
request. [Answer a real question](/first-answer) walks one research flow end to
end: filter companies, open one, and list its funding rounds.
